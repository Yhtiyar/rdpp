what we are building:
An app, for kids. Kids needs to earn points to unlock the screen time. All apps except essentials (could be set up by parents) will be blocked. By spending the points, kids get screentime. 10  pages reading = 100 coin.  100 coin = 15 minute screen time acess. The app should use builtin functionality to track and block app based on screentime for ios and android. 

The scenes concepts are in ./scene-images. The style should fully match. Do not simplify. Colors/images, gradients, fonts, everything based on style should match to these images. Verify in end, and redo/improve if do not match 90%.

Use that books for all ages.  All question-tests should be pregenerated and put into app. The reader should be good enough, to track how much % is readed, where left, ability to go back, etc. 

For MVP, we don't have any server/backend. Only app. For mvp, russian and english should be supported. By language I mean interface. However, if russian language is sellected, it should also include english books in (english books tab), and questions should remain in english
