# MiMi asset production

Built-in imagegen was used with `assets/art/mimi_face.webp` as the identity reference. The generated transparent 4×2 sheet was split with ImageMagick into eight consistently sized, padded 448×448 WebP files in `assets/art/mimi/`. The six stable poses are ready, thinking, encouraging, proud, celebrating, calm; additional ready/proud closed-eye frames support blinks. The outcome sequences use expression and paw frames, not whole-image bobbing. No remote assets or new runtime packages.

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: illustration-story. Create a production sprite sheet PNG with actual transparent background for littlewins' MiMi purple kitten. The reference is the exact identity: preserve fluffy lavender fur, very large glossy purple eyes, small pink nose, cream muzzle, pink inner ears, soft 3D lighting. 4 columns x 2 rows, exactly eight equal square cells, no text, no borders, no props outside cells. Same front-facing head-and-chest framing, identical head scale, eyes and ear alignment, safe margins in every cell. Row 1: ready gentle smile eyes open; thinking looking slightly up one paw by cheek; encouraging sympathetic warm smile slight head tilt; proud bright confident smile paw lifted. Row 2: celebrating joyful smile two little paws lifted; calm soft reassuring smile eyes open; ready blink CLOSED EYES same exact position as cell 1; proud blink CLOSED EYES same position and paw as cell 4. Distinct facial expression and paw movement, never sad or guilt-inducing. Consistent rendering and alignment for crossfading frames. Transparent alpha background, no checkerboard baked in, no lettering, ears and paws not cropped. Asset sheet only, 2048x1024 landscape.

Bundled sounds are reproducibly authored by `tools/prepare_feedback_sounds.py`: correct 220ms, retry 260ms, batch 700ms, milestone 780ms, time-ready 500ms. Amplitude envelopes soften the attack and decay; perceived quality and physical-device loudness were not evaluated.
